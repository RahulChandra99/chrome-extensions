Shred Log for Chrome

Click the button on any page and it goes into your Shred Log, already sorted into a topic. YouTube videos go to Watch later, and everything else goes to Saved links.

Getting started

  1. Pin the button. Click the puzzle piece 🧩 in Chrome's toolbar, then the pin next to Shred Log.
  2. Stay signed in to claude.ai in Chrome. Links are written through your Shred Log page, so they sync to your account like everything else in it.
  3. Click it on any page. It saves straight away. Tap Change in the popup if the topic is wrong.

If your Shred Log isn't open, it opens in a small pinned tab in the background to receive the link. Leave that tab open and links arrive instantly.

Where to send links

Your sorting rules

These run before the built-in sorting. Use a website (like bbc.co.uk/food) or a word or phrase (like skincare).

Try the sorter

Bookmarks bar

The extension adds a 📥 Shred Log folder to your bookmarks bar. Anything you put in it goes straight to your Shred Log:

  • Drag a bookmark onto the folder. It's sent, and the bookmark goes back to where it was.
  • Drag a whole folder onto it. Every link inside is sent, sorted one by one.
  • Click the ⭐ star on any page and pick 📥 Shred Log as the folder. The page is sent, with no extra bookmark left behind.

Chrome doesn't let extensions add items to the bookmarks bar's right-click menu, so this folder is the one-step way to do it. Chrome remembers the last folder you starred into, so switch the folder back in the ⭐ dialog when you want an ordinary bookmark.

Shortcuts

  • Right-click any link → Send link to Shred Log. Sent without opening it
  • Alt+Shift+S Save this page and open the popup
  • Alt+Shift+D Save this page without the popup

To change these, open chrome://extensions/shortcuts.

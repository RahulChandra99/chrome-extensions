Video to Notes

Everything here stays in this browser.

Claude

From console.anthropic.com. It's sent only to api.anthropic.com, and each set of notes is billed to your account. Any language. The video can be in a different one.

Notion

Each set of notes becomes a page under the page you pick, or a row if you pick a database.
How to get these
  1. Go to notion.so/profile/integrations and create a new internal integration. Copy its token.
  2. Open the Notion page or database you want notes in. In its menu, choose Connections and add your integration.
  3. Copy that page's link and paste it above.
If it's a database, columns named Category, Tags, Channel and a URL column are filled in when they exist.

Files

Notes are saved into a "Video Notes" folder in your Downloads.

Obsidian

Chrome asks once whether it may open Obsidian. Long notes travel through the clipboard.

Webhook

Gets a JSON POST with title, url, channel, category, tags, markdown and text. Works with Zapier, Make and n8n, which can pass notes on to Google Docs, Slack and the rest.

Link saver extension

Sends the video and a short summary to another extension that accepts them.
